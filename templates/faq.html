FAQs

Good questions, clear answers.

What the product does today, what the numbers mean, and how your data is handled.

Can I try it before making an account?

Yes. The live demo creates a short-lived, isolated demo workspace with synthetic data. Demo edits are separate from account data and can be reset.

How long is the free trial?

The default is seven days. An administrator can configure a duration from one to sixty days. Trial status is checked on the server, not just in the browser.

Are the construction rates current market prices?

No. Built-in cost allocations and any development assumptions are illustrative. Enter your own rate and replace assumptions with comparable quotations from your area.

Is the floor-plan editor professional CAD?

No. It supports a practical 2D room layout with saved dimensions and positioning. It is not certified architectural or structural software.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your existing information is preserved. New premium changes are paused until an eligible paid subscription is confirmed, while account and billing pages remain accessible.

Does a payment success page unlock a paid plan?

No. Subscription access is updated by verified server-side provider events. A return URL alone does not grant paid access.

Are my documents public?

No. Uploaded project documents are stored outside the public static directory and downloaded only after the server verifies access to the owning project.

Can I delete my account?

Contact the site administrator using the contact page to request account deletion. A self-service account-deletion workflow and retention controls remain a launch item.
