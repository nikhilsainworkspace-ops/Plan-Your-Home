About

Better home planning starts with clarity.

Plan Your Home brings the everyday information around a residential project into one place: the rooms, assumptions, materials, tasks, spend and documents that help people make decisions together.

Built for the planning journey

Homeowners should be able to understand the space and budget decisions without learning complex professional software first. Professionals should be able to keep project information organised and discuss the assumptions behind an estimate.

Honest about the numbers

Sample rates and example project information are identified as illustrative. A real estimate depends on location, design, specification, market conditions, soil, approvals and qualified local advice.

Useful tools, not professional certification

The planning editor is not a CAD or structural analysis system. It does not certify architecture, calculate code compliance or replace a licensed professional.
