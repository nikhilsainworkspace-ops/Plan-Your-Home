Privacy

Privacy policy

Starter policy text for review before launch.

Information stored

The application stores account details, project details, room geometry, estimates, materials, expenses, tasks, uploaded document metadata and account activity to provide the requested functionality.

Use of information

Information is used to operate the workspace, protect accounts, handle support requests, calculate planning summaries, and synchronise billing state when a payment provider is configured.

Access and storage

Project records are scoped to the signed-in account. Uploaded files are stored privately and served after an ownership check. The anonymous demo is maintained in a separate visitor-specific data namespace.

Service providers

Optional SMTP email and Razorpay billing are only used when configured by the site owner. Payment card details are not stored in this application.

Retention and requests

Account deletion and a complete retention schedule require operator procedures before launch. Contact the administrator through the contact form to request access, correction or deletion.

Before production

The operator must adapt this draft to the applicable jurisdiction, business entity, actual service providers, retention rules and legal advice. This starter text is not legal advice.
