How it works

Start simple. Add detail as you go.

Build a home-project record that can evolve from your first requirements into a practical working plan.

01

Define the project

Choose new build, renovation, extension, interior remodel or room refresh. Record plot dimensions, floors, built-up area and the budget you are working towards.

02

Shape the space

Add rooms to a 2D plan, adjust dimensions, and reposition them on the site grid. Save and return to the plan as needs change.

03

Build an estimate

Enter a cost-per-area assumption and contingency, then review the category split. Replace defaults with comparable local quotations when you have them.

04

Track and share

Record materials, expenses, tasks and files. Export a project CSV or PDF report for planning conversations.
